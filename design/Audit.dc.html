Why the site reads unserious

Read from the live source, then measured against body shops in Lima and premium paint shops abroad. The gap is not polish. It is that the palette, the type and the shapes belong to a consumer app, and the brand's own red never appears.

Today

1

A cream-to-white page

The body is a gradient from #e1dcbf to white. That is a bakery palette. Nothing in a paint shop is beige.

2

A sky-blue brand with a glow

Every button, link and icon is #0f76bd, and the hero sits under a soft blue radial. This is the look of a scheduling app.

3

The logo is red and black. The site is not.

The wordmark is a hard italic in #f7030d over black, with a car outline. None of that red appears anywhere in the stylesheet.

4

Poppins, and pills

Headlines are Poppins, the friendliest geometric face in the catalogue. Buttons are fully round at 999px and cards sit at 20px. Soft everywhere.

What the reference shops do

Germania Automotriz, Lima

Uppercase Montserrat 600 at 50px, white over a photograph darkened to 52 percent. Square corners throughout, steel greys, no colour but the photograph. The process is spelled out in five technical stages: digital measurement, cold pull, computerised colour match, oven, quality control.

Full Paint Perú, Lima

Near-black panels at #1c1c1c, one red accent, uppercase 700 headings, radii of two to four pixels. Opens on a before-and-after gallery of real cars and the list of brands it takes.

Premium paint and detail shops

Dark, high-contrast grounds, because paintwork only reads when there is something for it to reflect. One repeated graphic motif carries the whole identity. Proof lands before any self-description: a rating, a written warranty, the insurers they bill. One action, repeated down the page.

The pattern

None of them are friendly. They are competent. The reassurance comes from specificity — ovens, colorimetry, written warranties, the insurer's name — not from soft shapes and pastel colour.

What all three directions keep

Red and black

Taken from the wordmark that already exists, tempered per direction. The blue and the cream go.

Hard edges

Zero to two pixels of radius. Panels, not cards. No pills anywhere.

The work, photographed

Marked slots below until real photographs of the shop, the oven and finished cars exist. They are the single biggest lift available.
